      The rules, nice and simple

      Your turn. Your choice. One last balloon.

      The game is easy to learn, but the winning pattern takes a little thought. Here’s how a round works.

      01

      Pick your opponent

      Play a local, turn-based match with a friend sharing your screen, or take on the chameleon. Hard AI follows the optimal pattern; Easy AI chooses a legal move at random.

      02

      Pop one to four

      On each turn, select at least one and at most four balloons. Tap a balloon to select it, tap again to undo, or use the quick-select buttons. Press Pop! to end your turn.

      03

      Avoid the final pop

      The player who removes the last balloon loses immediately. The other player wins. The final balloon is never a prize—if it’s your turn, you must pop it.

      A friendly hint: look for 5k + 1

      When it’s your turn, a count of 1, 6, 11, 16, 21, 26, 31, or 36 is a losing position if your opponent plays perfectly. From other counts, remove just enough balloons to leave one of those numbers behind. For example, from 18, take 2 and leave 16. Your opponent can take 1–4; you can answer with the number that makes the pair add up to 5. Keep returning the board to 5k + 1, and the other player eventually has to pop the last balloon. It’s a pattern, not a guarantee against every human move—but it’s a powerful place to start.

      These are 5k + 1 target counts. From 17, take 1 to leave 16; from 18, take 2; from 19, take 3; from 20, take 4. If your turn starts on a target count, there is no guaranteed winning move—take 1 and hope your opponent makes a mistake.

      About Chameleon Arcade

      A bright little break for curious minds.

      Chameleon Arcade pairs its original misère Nim balloon game with six tougher algorithm trials: Hex, constrained network sabotage, evolutionary maze running, A-star labyrinth building, a cellular outbreak, and an expert code-breaker.

      Play solo or share the screen where supported. Games run in your browser; no account or sign-up is needed. Use Your trail to save a private checkpoint for any of the seven games on this server.
